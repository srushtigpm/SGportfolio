## What it is

Balcony Buddies is a small app for looking after the plants on my balcony. Every plant is a buddy with a name, a face and a mood, from Ruby the anthurium to Sir Hiss the snake plant. Each has its own care plan for water, light, food, and what to watch out for.

## How it plays

The **Today** tab is a list of care quests: water this one, mist that one, turn a pot, check for pests. Finishing them earns XP, keeps a streak going, and levels the balcony up from Seed to Sprout and beyond, unlocking little decorations like fairy lights along the way.

Buddies react to how they're looked after. They hop when they're thriving, droop when they're thirsty, and ask for help in a speech bubble when something's off. Photo check-ins build an album for each plant, so you can see it grow week by week.

## What I like about it

It makes a chore feel like visiting friends. Open it, see who needs a drink, and the balcony gets a little greener.
